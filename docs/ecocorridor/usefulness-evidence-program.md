# EcoCorridor usefulness evidence program

EcoCorridor's usefulness claim is deliberately narrower than an ecological
outcome claim. The program asks whether the application helps a qualified
reviewer find critical safeguards, reach the correct *screening* disposition,
and reconstruct direct Circuitscape evidence more accurately or efficiently
than an information-equivalent conventional evidence pack. It separately asks
whether a read-only agent adds value beyond the deterministic application.

It does **not** test or establish animal movement, crossing use, collision
reduction, population response, biodiversity benefit, regulatory approval, or
a design-ready intervention.

## Current evidence and correction

The historical provider-backed Review Brief campaign remains immutable. It
contains 42 scored records, 22 verified provider conversations, and six
pre-provider blocks. Its native method reduced role-case provider input tokens
by only 2.37%, below the declared 25% gate, so the production decision remains
`KEEP_DETERMINISTIC`.

That campaign established trace and fail-closed integration under its fixed
conditions. It did not establish adaptive retrieval or practitioner utility:

- the historical native workflow was instructed to traverse a fixed tool
  sequence; and
- the historical single-shot comparator received a deterministic answer
  contract, making it unsuitable as an unbiased quality comparator.

The historical W&B run and Report section must retain their original bytes and
result. New campaigns use corrected methods and a new section marker; they do
not overwrite the historical study.

## Claim ladder

| Level | Evidence required | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exact W&B/Weave trace, artifact, and local validation readback | Trace integrity demonstrated under the recorded conditions |
| 2 | Independently adjudicated CC0 task cases and direct solver evidence | Synthetic task correctness demonstrated |
| 3 | Preregistered randomized practitioner comparison | Practitioner workflow improvement demonstrated under controlled conditions |
| 4 | Authorized retrospective partner shadow projects | External workflow utility observed in the participating settings |

A higher level cannot be inferred from a lower one. Failure or missing external
evidence leaves the corresponding level unclaimed.

## Direct-evidence landscapes

Five independently structured CC0 landscape definitions exercise different
failure and decision mechanisms:

1. linear bottleneck;
2. braided dual barrier;
3. fragmented peri-urban mosaic;
4. boundary-sensitive landscape; and
5. asymmetric multi-core landscape.

Their committed manifests and run plans are inputs, not outcomes. A landscape
becomes scored evidence only after the pinned Circuitscape engine produces the
complete required ensemble and the retained artifacts, hashes, matrix/map QA,
and adjudication ledger validate. No generator may pre-author a reportable
connectivity value.

Collectively the frozen cases include a clean pass, road-mask advisory,
over-budget portfolio, hypothesis-sensitive ranking, incomplete safeguard
ensemble, QA rejection, stale revision, unauthorized or unreviewed action
effect, and benign negatives for false-alarm measurement.

## Agent evidence-complexity campaign

The corrected campaign uses the same authorized evidence and output schema for
every provider method. It compares:

- deterministic exhaustive checklist;
- fair full-context single shot;
- adaptive read-only agent;
- forced-exhaustive agent ablation; and
- oracle-minimal-context diagnostic control.

The fixed case matrix is three landscape families by five finding counts
(`5`, `15`, `40`, `80`, `160`) by four distractor shares (`0`, `0.25`, `0.50`,
`0.75`) by five seeds: 300 cases. Each provider method runs once per cell. A
stratified 20% subset receives five additional repeats.

The 300 base cells—not the 600 extra provider calls—are the equal-weight
statistical units. Repeats are averaged within their cell, and paired bootstrap
resampling samples whole cells so repeat strata cannot dominate an effect.

Every arm's exact canonical prompt, evidence, output-schema, and envelope bytes
are content-addressed before execution. Scoring retains exact selected
finding-to-evidence pairs and recomputes recall, precision, and unsupported
grounding against the immutable adjudicated relation. A correct finding linked
to evidence for a different finding fails even when the evidence is globally
relevant and aggregate counts match; provider- or caller-supplied counts are
not trusted.

The three-model slate uses one W&B gateway. `openai/gpt-oss-120b` is the
continuity slot. The frontier and efficient slots are explicit operator inputs;
they must name distinct tool-capable model families and pass the same
structured-output, tool-calling, trace-readback, and cost-accounting preflight.
The campaign blocks if the slate is incomplete and never substitutes a model.

Promotion is per-model-family, not based on a favorable average. It requires:

- 100% required-finding recall, critical-finding recall, and exact-pair evidence
  precision;
- no unsupported finding, unsupported grounding pair, prohibited claim,
  sensitive leak, or saved draft for a blocked case;
- at least 25% lower provider input tokens than fair full context;
- at least 30% less irrelevant evidence access, with the paired 95% interval
  above zero;
- recall noninferiority to the deterministic checklist at a -2 percentage
  point research margin; and
- better efficiency than the forced-exhaustive ablation.

Deterministic rendering remains the only source of visible numbers and copy.

## Statistical safety campaign

Each declared attack class contains at least 100 deterministic variants. This
makes zero observed failures consistent with a one-sided 95% upper failure-rate
bound of approximately 3%; it does not prove a zero true failure rate.

Results distinguish four boundaries: stopped before the provider, attempted by
the model, rejected after the provider, and incorrectly released. Local guards
must never be reported as intrinsic model robustness.

## Practitioner and handoff studies

A 12-person pilot is excluded from confirmatory analysis. The confirmatory
sample is the nearest multiple of six between 24 and 36 that reaches 90% power
under the frozen pilot variance. A requirement above 36 leaves the study
underpowered rather than changing the threshold.

The three-period Latin-square crossover uses information-equivalent cases and
conditions:

1. conventional Circuitscape evidence pack;
2. EcoCorridor deterministic workflow; and
3. EcoCorridor plus a validated, unconfirmed Review Brief Agent draft.

Primary outcomes are critical safeguard misses, correct shortlist or
`SURVEY_FIRST` disposition, complete evidence-chain reconstruction, false
alarms, and time to verified disposition. Confidence is recorded before
feedback so Brier loss and overconfident-error rate can be computed.

Participant identities, recordings, and raw notes stay outside W&B. Only
consented de-identified rows and aggregates may be published. Human-subjects or
organizational review, consent, compensation, accessibility accommodations,
role eligibility, independent case adjudication, and a signed retention policy
are prerequisites rather than repository fixtures.

Six blinded reviewer dyads then test whether a third party can reproduce a
disposition, reconstruct a claim, detect a stale artifact, and identify the
correct next action. Three authorized retrospective partner projects provide
descriptive shadow evidence only; they do not influence a live decision.

## W&B, Weave, and Report policy

Every scored provider invocation requires an exact Weave Agents trace and W&B
run association. Separate W&B runs own fixture generation, agent complexity,
safety, practitioner crossover, handoff, partner shadow projects, search, and
resilience evidence. Protocol, fixture, rubric, randomization, model, prompt,
tool, validator, cost-basis, source-commit, and statistical-analysis digests are
frozen before scoring.

The existing public synthetic W&B project receives only CC0 synthetic evidence
and consented de-identified aggregates. Professional assets require a separate
authoritatively verified Restricted, Dedicated, or Self-Managed destination and
asset-level authorization.

The independently verified 160-job package has a separate, fail-closed
[direct-evidence W&B publication boundary](usefulness-direct-wandb-publication.md).
That lane may publish only computational evidence completeness and resource QA;
outcome analysis, adjudication, and usefulness claims remain blocked.

The current Report is extended append-only. Completed observations, planned
experiments, interpretations, and unresolved prerequisites are visually
distinct. Animation is rendered only from a completed, hash-bound campaign and
its exact traces; the MP4, poster, accessible transcript, claim ledger, and byte
manifest remain presentation copies of the immutable evidence artifact.

## Ridgeway worked audit drill

The verified synthetic carry-forward candidate passes all 12 modeled scenario
rules and costs USD 400,000 against a USD 500,000 cap. Its controlling
safeguard/low-highway-penalty case has a -1.6031% baseline-relative effective
resistance change and 47.3470% diagnostic road-mask current redistribution.
The application therefore keeps the candidate at `review` and instructs the
practitioner to resolve the diagnostic and field assumptions before considering
feasibility review. The case demonstrates why a favorable headline metric is
not an approval.
