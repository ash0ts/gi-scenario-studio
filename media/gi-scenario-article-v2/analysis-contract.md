# Analysis contract

## Reader's question

Why add conversation to a stormwater evidence tool, what would it need to do,
and what did the completed test tell us about the proposed implementation?

## Narrative

Start with a practical engineering decision: whether a GI option meets the
requirements for closer site review. Define volume and peak flow before showing
Cedar Trace's inexpensive failing option. Introduce the Evidence Rail through
the records an engineer must connect, then the proposed Guide through the
unmeasured effort it might save.

Explain why we narrowed the model's job to choosing a predefined answer
structure. Test that remaining choice by changing relevant evidence, irrelevant
evidence, and evidence availability. Report case-level results before operational
counts. Use one trace pair to locate the observed failure, then return to the
unanswered engineering-usefulness question.

## Reader takeaway

The tested configurations did not reliably select the expected plan when
prepared synthetic evidence changed. Their recorded disposition remained
disabled. The deterministic evidence path remained available without a model.
Whether conversation reduces an engineer's interpretation effort is unmeasured.

## Fixed, varied, and measured

- **Fixed:** repository-owned answer content, temperature 0, seed 17, one
  transport attempt, two shuffled opaque-ID/order presentations per scenario.
- **Varied:** deciding value, distractor values, target availability, and model;
  128 versus 256 output tokens in a separate response-contract diagnostic.
- **Measured:** rule-crossing response, unrelated-value stability,
  missing-target handling, strict response-contract completion, trace
  acknowledgement, latency, and provider-reported tokens.
- **Behavioral unit:** a unique scenario; both presentations must pass.
- **Operational units:** provider invocations, responses, and acknowledged
  traces, reported separately in methods notes.

## Evidence and scope

The fixture, saved analysis, attempt records, and article summary are the
numerical authorities. Source hashes are included in the media manifest.
The methods notes retain 672 = 384 + 288 calls, the 192/192 deterministic
control, token diagnostics, and publication recovery without new model calls.

Do not infer hydraulic validity, ecological benefit, field performance,
retrieval quality, hostile-prompt resistance, production safety, general model
rankings, or practitioner usefulness from this experiment. Do not relabel the
family-separated validation as an untouched or sealed hidden test; the source
records do not establish that tuning history.

## Editorial acceptance

A first-time reader should be able to explain the engineering problem, the
proposed role of conversation, the three experimental checks, and why the
tested configurations remained disabled. Connected paragraphs should carry
decisions and consequences without slogans or invented builder anecdotes.
Desktop and mobile previews must keep tables, captions, figure labels, and
evidence links readable and in order.
